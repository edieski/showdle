    How to play

    Guess the mystery musical. Each guess prints a strip of eight tickets that compare its credits with the answer.

    • Green: exact match
    • Striped yellow: partly right, or within 5 years
    • Plain with a punched hole: no match
    • Dashed outlines: not known yet, in the strip that sums up your guesses

    Under a wrong year, "try later" or "try earlier" says which way the answer lies.

    Example: the answer is Hamilton and you guess Dear Evan Hansen.

    Stuck? A song clue unlocks after 4 guesses, and the shape of the title after 7. The daily show is the same for everyone. Practice rounds are unlimited.
